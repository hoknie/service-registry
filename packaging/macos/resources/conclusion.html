svc-registry is installed

It needs a Postgres 15+ database. Put its address into DATABASE_URL in
~/.config/svc-registry/svc-registry.env
and restart the service:
launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/io.github.hoknie.svc-registry

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080 and sign in as admin@example.com; the password is in ~/.config/svc-registry/initial-password.

The service starts at every login; its log is ~/Library/Logs/svc-registry.log. Another user of this Mac sets it up with /usr/local/libexec/svc-registry/setup. To remove it: sudo /usr/local/libexec/svc-registry/uninstall.
